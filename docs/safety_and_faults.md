# Safety and faults

Modes are `normal`, `degraded`, `emergency`, and `controlled_shutdown`. Sensor disagreement, short projected survival, air-circulation loss, or breached reserves move operation conservatively. Two consecutive healthy observations are required to return to normal.

Fault events, candidate validation results, fallback use, deferrals, and physical deficits are structured records with reason codes. Deficits are reported rather than compensated by clamping inventories or inventing resource supply.
